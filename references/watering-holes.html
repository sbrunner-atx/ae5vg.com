Notes

  • These are voluntary IARU band plans and widely used conventions. They are not regulations. They differ by region, so check them against your national rules.
  • The weak-signal mode frequencies are harmonized worldwide.
  • On 20 m, FT4 now uses 14.080, the traditional RTTY watering hole. Most RTTY activity is now about 3 kHz higher.
  • Keyboard-mode QSOs gather within a few kHz above the PSK31 watering hole. RTTY and the other data modes spread out to about 10 kHz above theirs.

Sources

IARU Region 1 HF band plan (2020), ARRL Considerate Operator's Frequency Guide, IARU Region 3 band plan (2019). Please send corrections through the fldigi-mcp issues page.
