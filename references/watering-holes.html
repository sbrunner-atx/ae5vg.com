Notes

  • These are voluntary IARU band plans and widely used conventions, not regulations, and they differ by region, so verify against your national rules.
  • The weak-signal modes are globally harmonised.
  • On 20 m the traditional RTTY hole at 14.080 now sits under FT4, and real RTTY activity is found about 3 kHz higher.
  • Keyboard-mode traffic clusters within a few kHz above the PSK31 hole, and about 10 kHz up for RTTY and the other data modes.

Sources

IARU Region 1 HF band plan (2020), ARRL Considerate Operator's Frequency Guide, IARU Region 3 band plan (2019). Corrections welcome on the fldigi-mcp issues page.
