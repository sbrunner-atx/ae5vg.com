Mode identification

A cheat sheet for reading the waterfall, from the signature table fldigi-mcp's signal hunt uses. Waterfall images courtesy of the Signal Identification Wiki; each links to the mode's wiki entry with more samples and audio.

Telling the look-alikes apart

CW and BPSK31 can show the same 30 Hz line. The difference is keying: CW has gaps of 40 ms or more where the carrier is simply off; PSK never stops. Hopping modes are recognized because energy sits at discrete tone positions from one 30 ms frame to the next, which is exactly what the waterfall's dots are.

What the spectrum cannot settle

Two pairs cannot be separated by the spectrum alone: Contestia and Olivia, and THOR and DominoEX. The station's RSID burst settles it.
