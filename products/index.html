The toolset

Products

The connectors are rig-interface cables between an assistant and the programs you already run. Nothing about your fldigi, WSJT-X or N3FJP setup changes; the assistant plugs into the interfaces they already have.

Connector · GPL-3.0-or-later

fldigi-mcp

Every one of the 174 XML-RPC methods of fldigi 4.2.13, reachable through grouped tools. Signal hunting from the receiver audio, the Signal Browser over a proposed patch, band guidance, and the callsign transmit gate.

fldigi-mcp →
Connector · MIT

wsjtx-mcp

WSJT-X over its UDP message protocol: read decodes, answer a CQ, hand completed QSOs to the logger. FT8, FT4, JT65, MSK144, Q65 and WSPR.

wsjtx-mcp →
Connector · MIT

n3fjp-mcp

Logging to Amateur Contact Log and the 100-plus N3FJP contest loggers over their shared TCP API, with a verified log_qso flow and a tiered safety model for the log database.

n3fjp-mcp →
Connector · MIT

serial-console-mcp

AI on the serial console: routers and switches, transceivers over CI-V and CAT, rotators over GS-232, and anything else with a serial port. Verified live on a Juniper EX2200.

serial-console-mcp →
Utility · MIT

mcp-host-bridge

A loopback-to-remote TCP/UDP relay. It pinches a hole into the sandbox so a local MCP server can reach a program on another host.

mcp-host-bridge →
Product · in development

The Ham Radio Model

A small language model that reads a contest exchange through noise the way an experienced operator does: qso-resolver and qso-mcp, both still under development. The capture rig that collects its training data reports on the dashboard.

The Ham Radio Model →
Proposed, not yet in fldigi

The Signal Browser methods (browser.get_channels, browser.clear) and the RSID methods (rsid.get_hits, rsid.clear) are patches offered to the fldigi maintainer on w1hkj/fldigi issue 55 on 11 and 16 September 2026. They are not merged. On a stock fldigi the tools answer with a hint and nothing else changes.
