Recent segments

Decoded, the last three days

Every line the rig decoded that reads as traffic, segment by segment, garble included and nothing edited. A line counts as traffic when it holds several recognizable words or ham shorthand in sequence, or a callsign with an allocated prefix next to DE or CQ or repeated, in a line that is mostly not junk. A decoder on an empty channel prints single letters with a callsign-shaped token now and then; that is left out, so a quiet segment shows nothing. Each card names the KiwiSDR and the dial frequency; the slice is the 3 kHz above it, where the watering hole sits.

These are our own recordings of the public amateur bands through public KiwiSDR receivers, and they may be shared; amateur transmissions carry no expectation of privacy. Material contributed by other hams is handled case by case.

Source: a status file the rig publishes every two minutes, and day files rewritten on the same schedule, republished through a cache; a line can be up to five minutes behind.
